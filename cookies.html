What this policy covers

This policy explains how this website uses cookies and similar browser storage. Some storage is necessary for the website to operate or to remember your privacy choices. Optional analytics and marketing technologies must not be enabled through our consent controls unless you choose them.

Necessary storage

The site stores your privacy preference in your browser under sct_consent_v1. This allows the website to remember whether you accepted or rejected optional categories.

Core website or portal functions may also require technically necessary storage for security, authentication or session continuity.

Optional analytics and marketing

Where optional analytics or marketing tools are configured, the consent controls are designed so that tagged optional scripts run only after the relevant category has been accepted.

You can reject optional categories and continue to use the public website.

External websites

Links to third-party services take you to external websites. Those services may use their own cookies or storage once you visit them, subject to their own privacy rules.

Change your choice

You can also clear this site's browser storage through your browser settings. The consent banner will then be displayed again.
